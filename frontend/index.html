Streamlined Process

How NourishNet Works

A closed-loop, safe, and transparent workflow connecting generous donors with organizations serving people in need.

1
🍱

Donor Lists Surplus

Donors register surplus cooked meals or packaged food, specifying quantity, food safety consume-before deadlines, allergens, and storage requirements.

2
🤝

Recipient Claims Food

Shelters and NGOs browse verified available batches and claim full or partial quantities. The system locks batches atomically to prevent duplicate allocations.

3
🚚

Volunteer Accepts & Routes

Eligible volunteer drivers check vehicle capacity and accept delivery jobs. The system runs Dijkstra's algorithm to calculate the shortest road path.

4
🔐

Handover Verified via OTP

Upon arrival, the recipient provides a secure 6-digit Handover OTP. The backend validates the code and safely marks the delivery as completed.

Engineering Highlights

Built with Intelligent Algorithms

Combining computer science fundamentals with social impact to eliminate food waste efficiently.

🗺️

Dijkstra Road Routing

Calculates the true shortest route over a weighted road junction graph using Java's PriorityQueue, avoiding traffic bottlenecks and reducing transit time for perishable meals.

📏

Distance Transport Policy

Uses Haversine spherical math to enforce business transport rules: trips ≤ 15 km are matched with local volunteers, while longer distances notify recipients to coordinate dedicated transport.

⏰

Strict Food Safety Lifecycles

Automatic background checks prevent expired food from being claimed or delivered. Batches past their safe consumption window are responsibly diverted to organic recovery.

♻️

Circular Food Waste Recovery

Unclaimed or spoiled batches are systematically tracked for composting and biogas processing, ensuring zero organic waste reaches local landfills.

🔑

OTP Handover Verification

Eliminates lost or disputed deliveries. Food batches can only transition to DELIVERED status once the driver inputs the recipient's authenticated 6-digit handover token.

⚡

Thread-Safe Architecture

Backend synchronization guards against concurrent race conditions during partial allocation claims, ready for seamless migration to Supabase PostgreSQL.
